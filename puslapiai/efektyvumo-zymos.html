How a picker’s reduced efficiency and paused zones are shown, as implemented in the prototype (2026-11-03). “Reduce efficiency” lowers efficiency for auto tasks for the whole account (all zones) when the picker is unwell, and stays until the shift manager removes it. Zones are paused one by one (e.g. no heavy goods after an injury). Both are managed only in the picker’s account. Raising efficiency is a wave boost on the Dashboard; a new picker’s numbers are changed in Edit.

Reduced efficiency is red; a paused zone is grey. There is no end time – the manager removes it.
