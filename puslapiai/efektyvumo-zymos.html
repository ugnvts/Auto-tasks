Efficiency adjustment tags

All states of efficiency adjustments and paused zones, and the places where they are shown, as implemented in the prototype. An adjustment always belongs to a picker’s zone; an action for several pickers (in the table or the “Temporary changes” panel) applies the same zone adjustments to each of them.

Colour shows the direction: decrease increase; a paused zone is grey. Validity: limited shows “until 14:00”, indefinite has no label. The “Working now” card shows no adjustment or paused zone tags; the full picture is in the “Temporary changes” panel.
